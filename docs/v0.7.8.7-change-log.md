# Infinite Realms v0.7.8.7 — Change Log

## Political world-map and location integration

- Added a responsive “Political Geography” summary to the existing World atlas/index and concise territorial information to Regional/Local location inspection.
- Displays political name/type and resolved capital/government/leadership information only where supported and allowed by discovery state.
- Distinguishes explicitly unclaimed regions from unknown or unresolved ownership.
- Political information is read-only and adds no travel/action controls.
- Does not draw political borders without explicit boundary polygon geometry. No synthetic borders are inferred from site positions, roads, adjacency, or ownership.
- Updated the existing atlas fallback copy to say “No political boundary geometry recorded,” avoiding the misleading implication that political entities do not exist when country/polygon geometry is simply absent.
- Preserves the existing World/Regional/Local controls and existing travel preview/confirmation actions.

## Generation and save integration corrections required for useful display

- Fixed `js/game/55-world-political-generation-v0.7.8.6.js`, which previously rolled back all political records because it selected invalid capital/region references and placed political IDs in the geographic `countryId` field.
- Fresh worlds now receive deterministic metadata-only subregions and up to three political entities after existing sites are available. Capital candidates come only from settlement sites assigned to the corresponding region.
- Political IDs are not written into geographic country references; ownership/governance is stored on regional political references.
- Existing non-empty political registries are not regenerated. Existing saved worlds without political data take the conservative initialization path and keep valid pre-existing region assignments instead of being repartitioned.
- Transaction-like rollback restores political and regional metadata if new political records fail validation.

## Physical geography and gameplay

- No physical sites, location IDs, coordinates, biome/terrain/type fields, global routes, or per-site travel connections are intentionally changed by the political generator.
- No political actions, wars, conquest, diplomacy, faction membership, or dynamic border movement.
- No save schema version bump or new database/framework.
