## Known limitations

- Interactive live-browser behavior was not verified because the available headless Chromium workflow has previously timed out even for `about:blank`; this report does not claim live-browser acceptance.
- No player-exported historical save was supplied. Save compatibility was tested through the existing synthetic legacy-save fixtures and production normalizer.
- The Desert/river reconciliations are conservative lore/data choices, not a simulated monster ecology. There is no breeding, migration, population simulation, territorial AI, or new combat mechanic.
- Explicit scripted `startCombat(enemyId, source)` is intentionally not blocked by the biome layer. That is necessary to preserve dungeon bosses, sparring, story events, targeted NPC fights, and other existing explicit choices. Normal random selection is habitat-filtered.
