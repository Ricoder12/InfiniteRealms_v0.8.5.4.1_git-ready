# Infinite Realms v0.7.6.4 — Racial Biome Affinity Reference

## Design rules

- Uses the eight existing races and the nine authoritative biome IDs from `IR_BIOMES`.
- A biome not listed as strong or familiar is neutral for that race. Human is the exception: adaptability applies lightly in all nine biomes.
- Affinity is a bonus, never an access requirement. It does not change race stats, skills, progression, racial talent trees, map generation, or travel availability.
- Current location is resolved through the existing biome resolver. Unknown or fallback biome references receive no modifier. Unknown/corrupt race IDs also receive neutral modifiers and do not inherit Human adaptability.
- Affinity modifiers come from one shared resolver (`ir0764AffinityForSite`) and one shared modifier table (`IR_V0764_AFFINITY_MODIFIERS`).

## Eight-race affinity matrix

`Strong` = +4 percentage points to gathering success and -1 energy from an applicable recurring environmental hazard tick. `Familiar` = +1.5 percentage points to gathering success. `Adaptive` = Human's +1 percentage point to gathering success in every biome, with no hazard reduction. `Neutral` = no affinity modifier.

| Race | Forest | Plains | Mountain | Swamp | Desert | Snow | Coast | River | Woodland |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive | Adaptive |
| Dwarf | Familiar | Neutral | **Strong** | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Familiar |
| Elf | **Strong** | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Familiar | Neutral | Familiar | **Strong** |
| Beastkin | **Strong** | **Strong** | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | Familiar | Neutral | Familiar | **Strong** |
| Orc | Neutral | Familiar | **Strong** | Neutral | Familiar | **Strong** | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral |
| Goblin | Neutral | Neutral | Familiar | **Strong** | Familiar | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral | **Strong** |
| Demonkin | Familiar | Neutral | **Strong** | **Strong** | Neutral | Familiar | Neutral | Neutral | Neutral |
| Slimefolk | Familiar | Neutral | Neutral | **Strong** | Neutral | Neutral | **Strong** | **Strong** | Neutral |

All other race-biome pairs are valid neutral combinations. `Cave` legacy locations resolve to the existing mountain biome and therefore use mountain affinity; no new cave biome was added.

## Modifier calculation

1. Resolve the supplied site through `irResolveBiomeProfile(site)`. A failed/safe-fallback resolution yields zero affinity modifiers.
2. Resolve the race's level for the resulting biome: `strong`, `familiar`, `adaptive`, or default `neutral`.
3. Add the level's gathering-success bonus to the existing `ir04Gather` success calculation, then preserve the existing final clamp of 0.35–0.96.
4. Dwarf mining receives no additional affinity chance bonus because the existing gathering formula already grants dwarves +0.10 chance. The dwarf mountain affinity still reduces recurring hazard energy loss where applicable.
5. When a hazard tick occurs, strong affinity reduces that tick's energy loss by one, floored at zero. It does not reduce hazard health loss. Existing Survival/armor mitigation is checked first and still prevents the existing hazard effect entirely.
6. Affinity is calculated from the current site's authoritative biome and is not stored in the character/save. Existing characters need no migration.

| Level | Gathering-success bonus | Hazard energy reduction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strong | +0.04 | 1 per applicable tick |
| Familiar | +0.015 | 0 |
| Adaptive | +0.01 | 0 |
| Neutral | +0 | 0 |

These are percentage-point additions to the existing chance formula, not multipliers. Affinity does not directly improve item quantity, quality, combat damage, movement speed, or skill gain.

## Existing racial trait overlap

- Dwarf mining's existing +0.10 chance is retained; affinity does not add another mining chance bonus.
- Existing dwarf deep-miner, elf green-hand, goblin scrap, beastkin exploration, slimefolk recovery, and magic/combat talents remain unchanged.
- Strong affinity only reduces the energy component of environmental hazards. It does not override Survival or armor logic and does not grant full hazard immunity.

## Compatibility and safety

- No affinity fields are added to saves, so save schema and normalization remain unchanged.
- No world generation, biome profiles, gathering pools, item definitions, or routes are changed.
- Neutral and unfamiliar biomes remain accessible; no negative affinity penalties are applied.
