# Infinite Realms v0.7.6.3 — Biome Hazard Reference

The authoritative hazard definitions are in `IR_V0763_BIOME_HAZARDS` within `js/game/39-environmental-hazards-v0.7.6.3.js`. Biome identity continues to come from the existing `IR_BIOMES` registry.

| Biome | Hazard | Minimum site risk | Tick interval | Effect if unprepared | Mitigation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Snow | Cold exposure | 20 | 7 exposed days | −1 energy and −1 health | Any existing equipped armor or Survival 5+ |
| Desert | Heat exertion | 20 | 7 exposed days | −2 energy | Survival 5+ |
| Mountain | Rough mountain terrain | 25 | 4 exposed days | −1 energy | Survival 4+ |
| Swamp | Boggy ground | 25 | 4 exposed days | −1 energy | Survival 4+ |
| Forest | Dense undergrowth | 35 | 5 exposed days | −1 energy | Survival 3+ |
| Coast | Exposed shoreline | 30 | 5 exposed days | −1 energy | Survival 4+ |

All hazards apply only at risky, unsheltered non-settlement sites. Plains, river, and woodland have no active hazard in this version. No new item, equipment slot, hydration meter, weather simulation, racial affinity, or survival-needs subsystem was added.

Hazards run after the game's existing time advancement. A per-site exposure ledger is saved at `world.v0763Hazards`; it prevents repeated rendering or save reloads from applying a tick. Old saves receive the ledger with the day cursor set to their current `daysLived`, so no historical days are replayed. Travel applies exposure to the route leg's origin because the existing travel system advances time before changing `world.siteId`.

Health is clamped to at least 1, energy to at least 0. Resting and settlement recovery remain usable. Current World/Adventure views and travel previews warn about relevant hazards.
