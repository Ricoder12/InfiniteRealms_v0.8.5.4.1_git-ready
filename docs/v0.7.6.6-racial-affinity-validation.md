## Modifier validation
- Strong affinity: +4 percentage points gathering success and reduces applicable recurring hazard energy loss by 1.
- Familiar terrain: +1.5 percentage points gathering success; no hazard reduction.
- Human adaptive: +1 percentage point gathering success; no hazard reduction.
- Neutral: no affinity modifier.
- Dwarf's existing mining racial bonus is not added twice.
- Affinity does not change base attributes, skills, racial traits, encounter access, or valid life paths.
- Invalid race IDs resolve to neutral modifiers rather than inheriting Human adaptability.

Result: PASS. All affinity levels are within the documented bounds and the modifier resolver uses the current site's canonical biome.
