## Structural audit sample

The generated world had zero continent records and zero country records. Coordinates ranged from 8 to 92 on each axis and no world-scale coordinate fields were present.

## Interpretation

These results validate the shared data access contract and regression safety in deterministic tests. They do not prove that the browser UI has been visually tested, nor that world-scale or local-scale geometry exists. The world map implementation remains intentionally out of scope.
